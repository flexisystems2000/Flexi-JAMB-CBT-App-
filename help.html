Frequently Asked Questions
How do I update offline questions?
Go to Update Questions from the home menu while connected to the internet. Tap on "Check & Update All Questions" to download the latest WAEC/JAMB question bank to your phone.
Can I take CBT practice tests offline?
Yes! Once you have synced questions at least once via the Update Questions page, all CBT exams and practice modes work completely offline without internet connection.
How does bookmarking work?
When reviewing questions during or after a CBT practice test, tap the bookmark icon to save tough questions. You can access and review all saved questions anytime from the Saved Bookmarks screen.
How is my average speed and accuracy calculated?
Your accuracy percentage is computed from the total number of correct answers over completed test attempts. Average speed measures the time spent per question in seconds across your CBT sessions.
What if I encounter an incorrect answer or typo?
You can reach out directly via our WhatsApp or Email support channels listed above with the question details and subject name. Our academic team regularly updates correction patches in the question database.
